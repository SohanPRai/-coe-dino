About CoE Cyber Security

Established in 2018, the Centre of Excellence in Digital Forensics and Cyber Security aims to address the growing threat of cybercrime. Our state-of-the-art lab is dedicated to providing a platform for students, researchers, and professionals to collaborate and innovate in the field of cyber security.

Core Values

Innovation, Integrity, Collaboration, and Continuous Learning. We build secure foundations for the digital future.

Our Mission

To cultivate state-of-the-art expertise in Cyber Security, Artificial Intelligence, and Cloud Infrastructure through academic excellence and industry partnerships.

Key Objectives

  • 🔒 Establish secure foundations for national digital infrastructures.
  • 🧠 Advance local research in Machine Learning and Computer Vision.
  • ☁️ Promote resilient, scalable containerized cloud infrastructures.

Our Vision

To establish a world-class center of research and development, pioneering secure digital transformation and cultivating next-generation technocrats.

Future Outlook

Empowering students and researchers to push the boundaries of technology, transforming the region into a key hub for cybersecurity and advanced computation.
